# Kling 2.6 director

Treat Kling 2.6 as a separate generation model. Do not reuse 2.5’s silent endpoint restrictions or 3.0’s capabilities without verification. Identify image-to-video, text-to-video or motion-control mode and exact provider tier; motion-control instructions do not automatically apply to image-to-video.

Current evidence is limited: [YEZZ same-input comparison](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=69JTpGCio3Y) reports good2.6 value on two examples, with3.0 more lifelike but still imperfect. At3:07–3:17 the creator suggests adjusting performance speed on2.6. This is a hypothesis to test, not a guaranteed equivalence to3.0. WaveSpeed cost/latency do not establish Higgsfield cost/latency.

For a shot, verify current endpoint fields, audio behavior, duration and estimate; then form a2.6-specific prompt and review criteria. No local2.6 render has yet validated a house prompting method. Preserve that uncertainty rather than filling it with another model’s rules.
